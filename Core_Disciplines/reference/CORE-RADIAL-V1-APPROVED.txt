WHAT THE APPROVED CORE LOOKS LIKE (structural ground truth)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Layout: 620px-max square machine on the left (~1.22fr), matte status card on
the right (~0.78fr). SVG viewBox 600x600, centre (300,300).

Concentric build, outside -> inside:
  r184  outer structural wall, matte plate, 12 radial divisions + 12 bolts
  r177  housing face line; recessed channel at r174
  r162-148  outer index ring — 36 rectangular teeth every 10deg (every 9th
            emphasised), static
  r142-120  primary rotating ring (slow CW): 16px track bed, 48 timing marks
            (major every 6th), 12 segment divisions, one crimson timing block
            that travels with the ring
  r114-102  secondary ring (slow CCW) with dashed mid-track; faint crimson
            surge arc rides it during the 20s surge
  r98   recessed engine plate: machining circles r86/r74, 12 construction
        lines, 8 mounts, 4 structural arms at 0/90/180/270
  r58   central hub plate + segmented gear housing (12 ticks)
  lower regulator at 215deg, d76 (housing r24, gear r14/t9)
  central gear r40/t18 + two offset gears r16/t8 at 45deg and 160deg (mesh
        distance 56), bearing r13/r7

Pointer: articulated linkage permanently mounted on the axle — drive gear
r15/t9, support link, articulation gear r8/t7 at joint 1, telescoping second
link (span 24->58), folding 68deg when retracted, crimson mechanical tip;
extends to radius ~84 (never reaches the nodes at r~234).

Nodes: nine 74x74 chamfered chips at 39% of container, every 40deg, titles in
two lines (CREATIVE/DIRECTION ... PROMPT/ARCHITECTURE) on fixed sides. Each has
a wall-mount + extending shaft + toothed joint coupling on the housing.
Idle chip = matte outer-bg; hover/lock = crimson chip, off-white icon.

Detail card: matte flip panel (light page -> #222328 card; dark page -> #e7e6e1
card). Standby: "ON STAND BY / Pick a node to explore". Active: module number,
Anton title, blurb, tag chips, three meshed gears (CONTROL / REPEATABILITY /
SYSTEM) with per-node direction/speed sets.

Motion to confirm in a live capture: rings rotating in opposite directions,
gears spinning at different rates, crimson timing block orbiting, pointer
unfolding on hover, exploded disassemble/reassemble on theme toggle, surge
impulse every 20s.
